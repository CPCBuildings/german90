How the voice lesson works

You learn the vocabulary and grammar before starting a conversation. ChatGPT begins in English, teaches the German questions, and only then asks you to answer. You can always request a hint.

Voice happens in the ChatGPT app, not inside this webpage. This app prepares and copies the lesson brief; you paste it into ChatGPT and switch to Voice. It cannot automatically read ChatGPT's assessment back into your progress.
