How to use this course

Do the day’s lesson in order. Speak every German sentence aloud. For recall drills, look at the English and produce German before revealing the answer. Six days each week introduce material; every seventh day is a review-and-speaking day. If you miss a day, continue where you left off rather than doubling up. Aim for 45–60 minutes daily. Use the built-in ChatGPT oral-session brief several times per week; once a week, make the voice session 20–30 minutes and treat it as your weekly oral check-in.
